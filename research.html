Doctoral research

The thesis applies Ontological Security Theory to Indian foreign policy and argues that strategic autonomy is not merely a policy preference but a continuity of identity and selfhood.

Key concepts include sovereignty hypersensitivity, minimum viable identity, and narrative labour.

Affiliation: North-Eastern Hill University, Shillong. Supervisor: Professor T. T. Haokip.
