About

Profile

Colonel K. C. Monnappa (Retd.) is a retired Indian Army officer, a doctoral researcher in Political Science at North-Eastern Hill University, Shillong, and a policy writer working on strategic autonomy, ontological security, and regional governance.

His work spans military service, public policy, consultancy, and academic research. He has served in infantry, special forces, army aviation, and staff appointments, and has focused on South Asia, the Indo-Pacific, disaster resilience, and the security questions of the North East.

Interests include the civic and land questions of Meghalaya, the cultural landscape of Coorg, and literary fiction.
