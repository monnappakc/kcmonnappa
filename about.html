About

Profile

Colonel K. C. Monnappa (Retd.) is a retired Indian Army officer, now a doctoral researcher in Political Science at North-Eastern Hill University, Shillong. He works on strategic autonomy and ontological security, and on the civil and security questions of the North East.

Before academic life, he served in infantry companies, in army aviation as a helicopter pilot, and in staff appointments at Army HQ, the Territorial Army Directorate, and Northern Command. He commanded in counter-insurgency and high-altitude areas of the North East and carried out disaster relief in Tripura.

His consultancy covers disaster management, exercise design, and policy writing. He has taught and trained in leadership, law and writing, and has written on the institutions and security questions of South Asia in both academic and public forums.

Interests include the civic and land questions of Meghalaya, the landscape and culture of Coorg, and literary fiction.
